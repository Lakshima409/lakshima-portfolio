Malcolm Lismore Photography

A cinematic digital experience designed to showcase Scotland through the lens of a freelance photographer.

I designed and developed a responsive, multipage photography website for a photographer specialising in Scotland's landscapes, wildlife, coastal scenes, weddings, and special events.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptPHPMySQLApache
01 · Overview

A portfolio and a practical business website

The goal was to create more than an image gallery. Visitors should be able to discover Malcolm's work and story, understand his photography services and pricing, read updates, and make an enquiry through one connected experience.

Photography stays at the centre of the visual design, while clear navigation and supporting information guide visitors from inspiration toward a potential booking.

My roleUI/UX design, web design, front-end and back-end development, database design
Project typeResponsive multipage photography website
Primary journeyDiscover → Explore → Learn → Evaluate → Enquire
02 · The Challenge

Make the imagery shine and the business easy to use

Creative portfolio

Present a distinctive photography identity rooted in Scotland's North West Coast, with large imagery and category-based gallery browsing.

Business experience

Make the photographer's story, service options, pricing, blog, and enquiry route easy to find without competing with the photography.

The gallery also needed an organisation that could accommodate future photography without becoming difficult to browse.

03 · Design Process

From requirements to a coherent site experience

The planning work connected client and user requirements to information architecture and fidelity wireframes before implementation.

Requirements
Information architecture
Wireframes
Interface design
Development & testing

Visual direction

Natural, cinematic, minimal, and immersive. Large photographic imagery establishes the atmosphere, with typography, spacing, and structured sections supporting the content.

User experience

Simple navigation, readable content, consistent page structures, and responsive layouts help visitors move naturally from exploring the portfolio to making an enquiry.

04 · Website Experience

One connected photography experience

The multipage structure brings the photographer's introduction, portfolio, services, editorial content, and customer enquiry journey together.

05 · Development

Custom-built web application

Front end

  • HTML for page structure and content
  • CSS for visual design, layout, and responsive behaviour
  • JavaScript for interactive functionality

Back end & hosting

  • PHP for server-side functionality and processing
  • MySQL relational database for website information and enquiries
  • Apache selected for the PHP hosting environment

The data model was planned around the site's information needs, including photography-related content, service and pricing information, and enquiry submissions.

06 · Testing & Quality

Checking the core visitor journeys

  • Navigation links and homepage content
  • About page readability and formatting
  • Pricing clarity and access
  • Gallery image display and category browsing
  • Enquiry form fields and submission flow

The project test plan documents checks across these core areas, including navigation, page content, gallery browsing, and enquiry submission.

07 · Outcome & Reflection

A complete journey from discovery to enquiry

The project brings photography, storytelling, and business functionality into one website. Visitors can discover Malcolm's work, learn about services, review pricing, and reach out about a booking.

What I learned

Planning a multipage experience, translating requirements into wireframes, balancing image-led design with clear information, and connecting front-end work with server-side and database considerations.

Future improvements

Advanced gallery filtering and search, expanded SEO, stronger security measures, and a content management system to support ongoing updates.
