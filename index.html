Research

Big Data, Marketing & Consumer Behaviour

Assessing Big Data's influence on marketing and consumer behaviour in the retail industry.

Research overview

A quantitative study examining how data-driven retail marketing relates to customer trust, segmentation, and engagement.

The study gathered questionnaire responses from 55 participants through Google Forms and analysed the results using SPSS and Excel.

Read the research report

Methods & analysis

  • Quantitative questionnaire research
  • SPSS and Excel analysis
  • Descriptive statistics and reliability analysis
  • Correlation and regression analysis

Reported findings

Pearson correlation of 0.85 for ethical practices and trust; regression reported an R² of 0.72 for ethical data practices and customer trust.
