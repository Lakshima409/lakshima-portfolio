PantryPal

AI-Powered Recipe Recommendation & Meal Planning System

📚 Final Year Research Project
🎓 London Metropolitan University
📅 2025

About My Final Year Research Project

My final-year research project, PantryPal – Recipe Recommendation and Personalized Meal Planning System, focused on investigating how artificial intelligence and machine learning could be applied to improve everyday meal-planning decisions.

The idea originated from a simple but common problem: people may have ingredients available at home but still struggle to decide what to cook while trying to maintain dietary requirements, nutritional goals and a reasonable grocery budget. Through my literature review and analysis of existing food recommendation platforms, I identified a gap in the integration of pantry management, personalized recipe recommendations, nutritional support and automated shopping-list generation.

System Design & Implementation

To address this problem, I designed and developed PantryPal as a full-stack web application. The system allows users to create profiles, specify dietary preferences, manage pantry ingredients, receive personalized recipes, generate weekly meal plans and automatically identify missing ingredients for shopping lists.

The technical architecture combines React.js for the frontend, Node.js and Express.js for the backend, MySQL for structured data storage, and Python/Flask microservices for AI functionality. The recommendation component applies machine-learning techniques including TF-IDF, cosine similarity, content-based filtering, collaborative filtering and neural embeddings, while contextual information such as time of day and season can also contribute to personalization.

Technology Stack:
React.js · Node.js · Express.js · Python · Flask · MySQL · Scikit-learn · TensorFlow · REST APIs · JWT · Docker · Spoonacular API

Development Approach

As the sole researcher and developer, I was responsible for the complete development lifecycle, from identifying the research problem and conducting the literature review through to system design, implementation, testing and evaluation. I used an Agile/Scrum approach to manage development through iterative sprints and continuously refine the system based on testing and feedback.

Key Features

  • AI-powered personalized recipe recommendations
  • Pantry and ingredient management
  • Dietary and nutritional personalization
  • Weekly meal-plan generation
  • Automated shopping-list generation
  • User authentication and profile management
  • Context-aware recommendations
  • Recipe favourites and interaction tracking

Skills & Learning Outcomes

Beyond the technical outcome, the project gave me valuable experience in combining research, artificial intelligence, machine learning, full-stack development, database engineering, API integration, cybersecurity, UI/UX design and project management within a single real-world application.

Most importantly, the project taught me how to transform a research problem into a working technological solution and how to evaluate that solution critically rather than focusing only on implementation.

Research Focus

The project explored the application of artificial intelligence and recommendation systems to everyday nutrition, meal planning and household food management, with particular emphasis on personalization, pantry awareness and reducing food waste. This research contributes to understanding how AI can make practical everyday activities more efficient and user-centric.
