Artificial Intelligence Research & Concepts

Overview

This research project explores fundamental artificial intelligence concepts through interconnected mind maps and comprehensive concept diagrams. The work synthesizes key AI domains and demonstrates a structured understanding of how intelligent systems think, plan, and interact with uncertainty.

Research Topics

  • Intelligent Agents: Autonomous systems, agent architectures, and decision-making frameworks
  • Pathfinding & Search: Adversarial search, game trees, and optimal solution strategies
  • Knowledge-Based Systems: Reasoning, inference engines, and knowledge representation
  • Natural Language Processing: Language understanding, parsing, and semantic analysis
  • Uncertainty & Probability: Probabilistic reasoning, Bayesian networks, and decision-making under uncertainty
  • Game Theory: Multi-agent systems, competitive and cooperative strategies
  • Human Augmentation: Collaboration between human intelligence and AI systems

Skills Demonstrated

AI Fundamentals · Systems Thinking · Research Synthesis · Knowledge Documentation · Conceptual Modelling
